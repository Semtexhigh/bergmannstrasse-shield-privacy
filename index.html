About this policy

Quiet Web (the "Extension") is a content blocker that removes advertisements and trackers as you browse. This policy explains what data the Extension does and does not handle, and applies to the Extension only.

Information we collect

The Extension does not collect any personally identifiable information. It does not collect or transmit your name or contact details, the pages or URLs you visit, your browsing or search history, your IP address, device identifiers or location, or any analytics or telemetry. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

How blocking works

Blocking happens in two ways, both entirely on your device:

  • Network blocking uses Chrome's declarativeNetRequest API. Rules are supplied to the browser and Chrome decides which requests to block; the Extension never reads request contents or logs the sites you visit.
  • On-page filtering uses content scripts that run on pages you open to hide ad elements and neutralise "disable your ad blocker" walls. These read and modify the page in your browser only.

No page content or browsing data is collected, stored remotely, or transmitted off your device.

Local settings & statistics

The Extension stores a small amount of data locally on your device via Chrome's storage API — your preferences, your paused-site list, and a local "items blocked" counter shown in the popup. This never leaves your device and is removed on uninstall.

Permissions

declarativeNetRequest
Apply the rules that block ad and tracker requests.
storage
Save your settings, paused-site list and block counter locally.
tabs
Read the active tab's address for per-site pause and redirect protection, and open the Extension's info tab.
Host access
On-page filtering runs on sites you open, solely to hide ads. Nothing is collected.

Network connections & remote code

Filter lists are bundled with the Extension and updated only when you install a new version from the Chrome Web Store. The Extension makes no runtime connection to any external server, loads no remote code, and sends no data over the network.

Sharing & third parties

Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing to share. We do not sell, rent, trade or transfer user data. There are no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

Donations

The Extension may display cryptocurrency wallet addresses for optional, voluntary donations. It does not process payments, record whether you donate, or collect any donation information.

Children's privacy

The Extension is general-purpose, not directed at children, and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
